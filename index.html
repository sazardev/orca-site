Orca

Your coding agents, in your pocket.

See what it does ↓ How it is set up
Scroll

The idea

Your agents keep working on your PC. Orca lets you look in, answer them and steer them, from wherever you are.

No cloud in between. No account. A small server next to the agents, your phone, and your own private network.

How it travels

Three hops. All of them yours.

  1. 1 · The agents stay homeClaude Code and OpenCode keep running inside Herdr on your PC. Nothing moves to a server farm.
  2. 2 · A small server speaks for themOne Python file with no dependencies reads their screens and conversations and offers a tiny API.
  3. 3 · Your phone asks over TailscaleHTTPS inside your own tailnet. The app polls it, notifies you, and sends your answers back.

What it does

Everything you would do at the desk, in the palm of a hand.

01

Every agent, at a glance

Agents are grouped by what you must do: the ones that need you first, then the ones working, then the ones that finished. Search across all of them, or switch to History.

  • Claude Code
  • OpenCode
  • Groups that stay collapsed
02

A chat, not a terminal

The real conversation: your words, its answers, its tool calls folded into “5 steps”. Markdown is drawn as written, code comes in blocks you copy with a tap, and Mermaid diagrams open to zoom.

  • Hold Send to change mode
  • Files open read-only
  • Diagrams
03

Questions, drawn as a form

When Claude asks several things at once, Orca takes the message field's place with steps on top, rows with boxes, a review and a Submit. Yes/no questions get plain buttons.

  • Pick one or many
  • An answer of your own
04

Answer from the notice

A permission question arrives with Yes and No right in the notification; a finished agent with a Reply field. They work with the app closed. Tapping opens exactly that conversation.

  • No push service
  • Stays inside your tailnet
05

One bubble for each agent

Float an agent and it follows you over any app, wearing its project's color and mark, with a dot for what it is doing. The + adds another conversation or a new project. Bubbles only show while you are outside Orca.

  • Project colors
  • Bar opens the app
06

Pick it up again

Every conversation Claude ever saved on the PC, in groups by day, with search, a project filter and an order. Resume starts Claude again in the folder it was had in, even if its agent was closed.

  • Search
  • Filter
  • Resume
07

Branches, side by side

A project opens to its conversations, its branches and the agents on each. A new branch gets a worktree of its own, so agents on different branches never step on each other.

  • Worktrees
  • Details
  • Git at a glance
08

A terminal, for a moment

Full screen, in the folder the agent works in, with colors and a row of keys. When you close it, Orca offers what happened so the agent knows what you did.

  • Esc · Tab · ^C
  • Closes by itself
09

Offline first, quietly

The last answers of your server stay on the phone, only in the app's own folder, and show with a quiet Offline line when the PC is out of reach. A page tells you what is kept, read and sent.

  • Your data
  • Forget buttons

By design

Calm, private, and honest about its data.

Private by construction

No account, no analytics, no third party. The phone talks only to your server, inside your own tailnet. Credentials never leave the PC.

Read-only unless you ask

Claude's history and OpenCode's database are only read. Branches, worktrees and settings change when you say so, and a Your data page shows every path and size.

Quiet, and offline-first

Notices only when something needs you or finishes. When the PC is out of reach the last answers still show, with a small line that says so.
