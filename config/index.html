The server

Everything is set with environment variables in the service unit. The defaults suit most PCs; only ORCA_ALLOWED_LOGIN is yours to set.

VariableDefaultMeaning
ORCA_PORT2588Port the server listens on, on 127.0.0.1 only.
ORCA_ALLOWED_LOGIN—The Tailscale login that may call the server. Without it the server refuses everyone.
ORCA_DEVoffSkips the identity check. For local development only; never behind Tailscale.
ORCA_CLONE_DIR~/WorkWhere new projects and clones go.
ORCA_STATE~/.local/state/orcaOrca's own two files: the pinned projects and the workspaces it started.
ORCA_HOMEyour homeThe only place projects may live. Hidden folders are left out.

CLAUDE_JSON~/.claude.jsonClaude's project list and MCP servers.

The app

Server address

The https address Tailscale gave you. Saved on the phone and handed to Android's side so the notice buttons and widgets work with the app closed.

Notifications

Notify me turns the background watcher on. Choose When an agent needs me and When an agent finishes separately. A notice reads project · Claude, then what happened in plain words. Yes/No and Reply work from the notice itself.

Saved on this phone

The last answers of your server, kept in the app's own folder for up to 14 days and 24 MB, and left out of Android's backup. Shown with a quiet Offline line when the PC cannot be reached. One button forgets them.

Setup check

Six lines that say what is wrong and open the right Android screen to fix it: address, internet, Tailscale, server, notifications, battery.

Appearance

Palette and mode

Ten curated palettes — Japandi (the default), Moss, Slate, Plum, Mono, Ocean, Ember, Rose, Nord, Midnight — each with a light and a dark variant, all checked for 4.5:1 text contrast. Or Material You from the wallpaper, or one generated from a color of your own.

Shape and density

Corners (sharp to round), border weight, and density (spacious, standard, compact) restyle the whole app at once.

Type

Geist by default, or the system font. Text size is its own slider and follows through everything.

Set Up

One place for each agent, from the phone.

Claude

Permissions in words — always allowed, ask first, never allowed — and the default mode, model and effort for new sessions. Plugins, hooks and variables are shown. MCP servers by place (everywhere, or one project): add a command or a remote one, remove one. Secret values are never shown or sent.

OpenCode

Its agents and what each may do without asking, its MCP servers and the providers it is signed in to. Read-only here: change them in OpenCode's own config.

Prompts

Your own presets for the ! shortcut at the start of a message: review, tests, fix, plan, commit, pull request, changelog and whatever you add.

Android

Widgets, the quick settings tile, launcher shortcuts and bubbles (one per agent, the notice as the agent's bubble).

Projects

Where they come from

Pinned on top, then Recent (where an agent worked lately) and Often visited (git repositories from your zoxide list).

A project's page

Start an agent; its conversations (worktrees included); its branches with the agents on each, and a new branch in a worktree of its own; and Details: git, conversations, 30-day use and the look of its bubbles.

New project, GitHub

New project makes a folder with git init in ORCA_CLONE_DIR, optionally with a private or public repository, and pins it. GitHub lists your repositories (through gh on the PC) and clones one in the background.

The look of a project

A color and a mark — a letter or two, or an emoji — for each project, shown in its bubbles. Left alone, they come from its name.
